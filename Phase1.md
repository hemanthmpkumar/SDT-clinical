Phase 1: Hypergraph Construction & Multimodal State SpaceThe objective of Phase 1 is to encode both the physician's cognitive path and the patient's physical state by dynamically tracking the evolving diagnostic hypothesis.  Node Initialization and Embedding FormulationThe raw query initiated by a clinician at turn $t$ is denoted as $q_t$.  This query is processed into a dense embedding $e_t = \text{Encoder}(q_t) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ using models like ClinicalBERT to preserve semantic geometry.  To keep matrix operations computationally efficient, the original 768-dimensional embeddings are projected down to a 128-dimensional subspace using a learned linear layer.  Covariance Elicitation and State TrackingInstead of maintaining a simple list of past queries, the instantaneous diagnostic hypothesis is modeled as a continuously evolving covariance structure over a sliding window.  The local intent at turn $t$ is represented by a symmetric positive definite (SPD) matrix.  The matrix is computed using the equation$$S_t = \frac{1}{k}\sum_{i=t-k+1}^{t}(e_i - \mu_t)(e_i - \mu_t)^\top + \lambda I_d$$.  In this formulation, $k$ is the sliding window size and $\mu_t$ represents the local mean of the embeddings within that window.  The inclusion of $\lambda I_d$ (where $\lambda > 0$) strictly guarantees positive definiteness, ensuring the matrix operates properly within its intended mathematical space.  Riemannian Manifold DynamicsThe state matrix $S_t$ resides in the manifold of SPD matrices $\mathcal{S}_{d}^{+}$.  This space is treated as a Riemannian manifold equipped with the affine-invariant metric tensor$$g_S(u,v) = \text{Tr}(S^{-1}u S^{-1}v)$$.  To accurately track semantic edge formation and topic drift, the system utilizes the scale-invariant geodesic distance$$d_g(S_{t-1}, S_t) = \vert{}\vert{}\log(S_{t-1}^{-1/2}S_t S_{t-1}^{-1/2})\vert{}\vert{}_F$$.  Finally, the system quantifies the evolving diagnostic uncertainty through the differential entropy of the Gaussian density, tracked as$$H(S_t) = \frac{1}{2}\log\det(2\pi e S_t)$$
